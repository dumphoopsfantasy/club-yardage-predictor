# Dump Golf — before / after

**Before:** Small, low-contrast yardages competed with closely packed rounded boxes. Club selection relied on dropdown stacks, and bag predictions were buried in secondary text.

**After:** A caddie's yardage book: larger printed-feeling tabular numbers, a 4px spacing rhythm, generous page gutters, fine rules instead of floating section cards, and restrained brass details. Clash Grotesk stays for its distinctive numeral shapes; regular weights balance the bold yardages.

Brand buttons and a searchable model sheet make catalog selection tactile. Known carry is a large, clearly labelled input. The bag is an ordered ledger with prominent editable yardages. Cream, charcoal, and fairway-green themes each use their own readable surfaces and accents. The launch screen uses the existing logo at a stable size rather than cropping a full-screen bitmap.

All catalog data, calculations, settings behavior, and existing shot inputs remain unchanged.
